About AstraWish

AstraWish is a Primogem tracking and savings management system designed for Genshin Impact players, especially beginners who want to monitor their daily Primogem earnings and stay consistent with their savings.

By manually logging your Primogems each day, you can maintain your tracking streak, monitor your progress, and calculate how much you need to save for your desired characters and weapons.

AstraWish also provides a navigation guide to help Travelers discover permanent sources of Primogems and maximize their potential earnings. Participating in limited-time events is also encouraged to earn additional rewards.

AstraWish was created with the help of AI integration. The ideas, design direction and decisions come from its developer, while AI supports the design and development of the system.

Please note: AstraWish does not automatically synchronize with your Genshin Impact account. All records must be entered manually. The system focuses exclusively on Primogems and Intertwined Fates, excluding other rewards such as Mora, Hero's Wit, and Enhancement Ores.

    About

    The dev and the system

    The developer

    AstraWish is a personal project built by a Genshin Impact player who wanted a calmer way to save Primogems and plan banners.

    AstraWish was created with the help of AI integration: the ideas and direction come from the developer, while AI assists in designing and building the system.

    Built by IGN Momobami.

    The system

    AstraWish tracks your Primogem savings, wishes, Intertwined Fates, streak, saving goal and character collection. Everything is calculated on your device and stored in your browser, so it keeps working after you close and reopen it.

    Version 1.1. Built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
